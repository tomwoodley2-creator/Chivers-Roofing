Privacy notice

How we use the details you give us when you ask for a quote or get in touch.

Last updated 18 October 2026

Who we are

Chivers Roofing Ltd is the data controller for the personal information described here. We're a limited company registered in England & Wales, company no. 17380327, with our registered office at 7 Gethin Grove, Crowthorne, RG45 6QD.

For anything about your data, email Tommy@chiversroofing.co.uk or call 07760 663711.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, phone number, postcode, the type of work and anything you write in the message box.
  • Phone, email or WhatsApp: your contact details and whatever you tell us about the job, including any photos you send.
  • If we carry out work for you: your address, contact details, quotes, invoices and payment records.

We don't ask for, and don't need, any sensitive information such as health details.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, visit and quote. You've asked us to, so the legal basis is taking steps before entering a contract with you.
  • To carry out and invoice the work. Legal basis: performing our contract with you.
  • To keep tax and accounting records. Legal basis: our legal obligations.
  • To keep the website running securely. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the site from abuse.

We don't sell your details, and we don't send marketing emails or texts.

Who we share it with

We only use a small number of service providers, and only so the website and our email work:

  • Web3Forms delivers enquiry form messages to our inbox.
  • Google Workspace provides our business email.
  • Vercel hosts this website and may keep short-term logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS content delivery network supply the site's fonts and styling. Your browser contacts them directly, which shares your IP address with them.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to message us there.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on the safeguards required by UK data protection law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK–US data bridge.

We'll also share information if the law requires it, for example with HMRC.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go ahead: deleted within 12 months.
  • Customer and job records: kept for 6 years after the work is finished, to meet tax rules and in case of any issue with the work.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, so there's nothing to accept or turn off.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information, where we don't have to keep it
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to us using it
  • send your information to you or another organisation in a usable format

Email or call us using the details above. It's free, and we'll reply within one month.

Making a complaint

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
