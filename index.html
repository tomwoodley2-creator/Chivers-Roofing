Our Roofing Services

Our team specialises in roof repairs, re-roofing and new roofing installations, working across a range of traditional and modern roofing systems.

Pitched Roofing

New roofs and complete re-roofing projects in slate and tile, including underlay, treated battens and mechanically fixed or dry-fixed ridge systems. We can also coordinate scaffolding and waste removal where required.

Roof Repairs

Professional repairs for slipped or damaged tiles, defective flashings, ridge work and roof leaks. We identify the underlying issue and carry out appropriate repairs designed to provide a durable solution.

Flat Roofing

Installation of new flat roofs using quality felt and single-ply systems, incorporating appropriate falls, upstands and trims to provide effective drainage and long-term performance.

Flat Roof Repairs

Repairs to damaged or ageing flat roofs, including blisters, splits, ponding and deteriorated felt. Where appropriate, we can undertake repairs, overlays or complete roof recoveries.

Lead Work

Specialist leadwork including valleys, soakers, chimney flashings and box gutters using Code 4 and Code 5 lead, installed with attention to detail and long-term durability.

Fascias, Soffits & Guttering

Replacement fascias, soffits and guttering in uPVC, installed with proper support, clean detailing and a professional finish.

Moss Removal & Roof Maintenance

Removal of moss from roofs, gutters and valleys, with a visual inspection for damaged, displaced or deteriorated roofing components. Any issues identified will be brought to your attention.
